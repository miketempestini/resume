Why Gantry

Stop prompting. Start assigning.

Your agents already write the code. Gantry gives them a real plan to work from, and gives you a board that shows what each one is doing.

Private by design

Gantry runs entirely on your computer. Every project is a file on your own disk, and there's no server anywhere holding your roadmap.

  • no cloud
  • no login
  • no account
  • one file per project

Industry-standard planning

Epics, features, stories, bugs and subtasks. A backlog, a board, a roadmap, sprints and releases, ready the moment you open it.

You stay in control

Agents claim a ticket before they start, ask before they change anything, and every edit they make lands in the activity log with Undo.

Bring your own agent

Your agents, your call

Gantry has no AI of its own and no servers to send your work to. It works with the agents you already use, so you decide where your ticket text goes.

When speed matters

Frontier models

Hand tickets to Claude Code, Codex or Cursor and put the strongest models to work. Your agent sends what it reads to the provider you picked. Gantry itself sends nothing anywhere.

Features

Everything a product team plans with.
Built for agents too.

01 / 04 · AI agents

Talk to your agents, right in the ticket

Mention @claude on any ticket and ask for help. The agent replies with a plan, waits for your yes, then claims the work and moves the ticket as it goes.

  • Agents claim tickets, so two never work on the same thing
  • Nothing changes until you reply yes
  • Every agent edit is logged, with Undo
  • Frontier or local, any MCP agent can join
placeholder clip

02 / 04 · Backlog and board

From epic to subtask, in one tree

Structure work as epics, features, user stories, bugs and subtasks. Edit right in the table, then drag cards across the board as work moves.

  • Inline editing for status, priority, sprint and dates
  • Filters and saved views that travel with the project
  • Bulk edit across any set of tickets
  • A Kanban board with your own statuses
placeholder clip

03 / 04 · Roadmap and sprints

See the whole plan on one timeline

A Gantt roadmap with a Today line, milestones and progress on every bar. Plan sprints by dragging tickets between them.

  • Drag to schedule, drag the edge to resize
  • Warnings for late work and dates that don't fit
  • Sprint planning with a No sprint list of what's left
  • Export the roadmap as a PNG or PDF
placeholder clip

04 / 04 · Releases

Release notes, written for you

When a release ships, your agent drafts a short note for each ticket from its own description, comments and commits. You approve it before anything is saved.

  • Notes come from the ticket, never invented
  • Edit any note, date or ticket before you share
  • Export a clean PDF to send anywhere

How it works

Every agent on its own ticket. Every claim visible.

  1. You ask a question you

    Mention @claude on a ticket, like you'd ask a teammate.

  2. The agent answers agent

    It reads the ticket and replies with a plan.

  3. You agree on a change you

    One click on Reply Yes, and it's approved.

  4. The agent updates the ticket agent

    It claims the work and moves the status as it goes.
