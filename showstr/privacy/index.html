Showstr

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 1, 2026

Your Data Stays On Your Device

Showstr stores your concert history and notes locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your data on external servers.

No Cloud Sync

Showstr does not sync data across devices. Your concert history is stored in the app on this device, and may be included in your device’s own iCloud or computer backups if you use them. You can export a PDF or CSV copy of your data at any time, but Showstr cannot import those files back into the app.

Photo Access

The app requests access to your Photo Library only to attach photos and videos to your concerts. They remain in your library - we only store references to them. You can revoke this permission anytime in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos.

No Tracking or Analytics

Showstr does not use any analytics, tracking, or advertising services. We do not collect information about how you use the app. We do not track your location, browsing habits, or any other personal information.

Exports and Copies

When you export or save a copy of your data as a PDF or CSV, files are created temporarily on your device and saved to your chosen location (Files app, AirDrop, Email, etc.). You have full control over where these files go and who has access to them.

External Links

The app may open your web browser to search for concert setlists using Google search. This uses standard web browsing subject to your browser’s privacy settings and Google’s privacy policy. We do not track or collect data from these searches.

Data Security

Your concert data is protected by your device’s passcode and encryption when your device is locked. No data is synchronized or backed up to external servers by this app. Your data is as secure as your device.

Data Retention

Your data remains on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual concerts, or use the “Delete All Data” feature in Settings to remove all concert data from the app. Deleting the app from your device will also permanently delete all data.

Children’s Privacy

Showstr does not knowingly collect any information from children. The app is designed for general audiences to track their personal concert attendance.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the app with an updated “Last Updated” date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access: View all your data in the app at any time
  • Export: Use the Export feature to download your data
  • Delete: Delete individual concerts or all data at once
  • Privacy: Revoke photo access permissions in iOS Settings
